# Free generic PDF templates

Blank authoring worksheets for adapting company-wide onboarding documents. Each PDF has a matching editable Markdown source. These are ordinary PDFs, not fillable signing forms.

**These generic templates have not been reviewed by a legal representative. They are not legal advice or production-ready agreements. Have a qualified legal representative review and adapt them for your organization and jurisdiction before live production use.**

## Repurpose a template

1. Copy the editable source or open the blank PDF in your preferred PDF editor. Markdown can be pasted into a document editor and exported to PDF.
2. Replace every blank/prompt with reviewed company-wide content. Add your company identity, document version, approval date, and appropriate contact information.
3. Have a legal representative review the content and how you intend to use it. The worksheets deliberately avoid prescribing binding compensation terms, employment status, waivers, or jurisdiction-specific clauses.
4. Export a new PDF and archive the exact approved original. Never populate it with an applicant's private details before uploading it as the public document.
5. Follow the [custom PDF setup guide](../../docs/custom-pdf-guide.md).

The plugin uses **one public PDF for all roles** at a time. It records applicant acknowledgement through a separate private WordPress form; it does not fill or sign these PDFs. If you combine sections from several worksheets into one document, have that combined document reviewed and assign it its own version.
